## **Welcome to the InfoSec Dojo**

**Where Code, Consciousness, and Community Converge.**  
Hello, I’m Kyle Ingersoll. Welcome to my digital dojo.  
If you are looking for a standard, copy-pasted list of cybersecurity CVEs or dry IT tutorials, you might be in the wrong place. The **InfoSec Dojo** is a space for holistic practitioners—software engineers, security architects, and systems thinkers who understand that true security is not just a technological challenge, but a deeply human and systemic one.  
We exist in a world of complex, rapidly evolving networks. Defending them requires more than just deploying the latest firewall; it requires a fundamental shift in how we view systems, code, and ourselves.  
My approach to Information Security and Software Architecture is built on three core pillars:

### **1\. Resilient Architecture & "Don't-Know Mind"**

Security cannot be bolted on as an afterthought; it must be woven into the fabric of the system. Drawing on principles from *Zero Trust Architecture* and the rigorous standards of the *AWS Well-Architected Framework*, I view network defense through the lens of mathematically sound system design. But great architecture also requires what Zen practitioners call "Don't-Know Mind"—an empty, receptive state that prevents our egos from blinding us to vulnerabilities. We drop our assumptions, verify everything, and engineer ecosystems that gracefully anticipate failure.

### **2\. Systems Thinking & Decentralized Trust**

Inspired by the mutualist principles of open-source software and complex systems theory, I view the digital landscape as a living ecosystem of human interactions. By applying *Thinking in Systems*, we analyze how power, data, and trust actually flow. True security empowers the user at the edge rather than concentrating brittle, authoritarian control at the center. We explore how open collaboration and mutual aid build far more resilient systems than walled gardens ever could.

### **3\. The Craft & The Daily Practice**

Ideas are meaningless without execution. Whether it’s writing *Clean Code*, designing a highly scalable distributed system, or refactoring a legacy application, the craft of software engineering requires overcoming resistance. A Dojo is a place of practice. This blog documents the raw, continuous work of turning up every day, refining the fundamentals, and communicating complex technical concepts with clarity and grace.

### **Join the Practice**

Information security is not a destination; it is a process of ongoing improvement. I created the InfoSec Dojo not just to share my own journey, but to build a community of peers who challenge, support, and elevate one another.  
Whether you are a senior architect designing cloud infrastructures, a junior developer writing your first API, or a thinker exploring the boundaries of techno-culture, there is a mat for you here.  
Take a breath. Drop your assumptions. Let's begin the work.
